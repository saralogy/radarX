Water purification

Boiling is the most reliable way to make water safe. Bring clear water to a rolling boil for one minute; at high altitude boil for three minutes.

Filtration

Let cloudy water settle, then pour it through a clean cloth before boiling or chemical treatment.

Chemical disinfection

Unscented household bleach can disinfect water when boiling is not possible. See also burn treatment and an external page.
